Parts and tokens

The studio look lives in the controls and pictures, never in the words. Every label says what the app does: Downloading, Queue, Rate limited, Hi-Res 24/96. Progress always has a number next to it.

Switches

Search covers every source by default. The Sources switch names what is searched and opens one rocker per source; a rocker is on when its top is pressed in. Lamps: green is on, red or yellow is on but failing, dark is off.

Ivory push buttons with capital legends. Amber is lit only on the main action of a screen; a selected switch is backlit peach. Buttons sink 2px when pressed.

Rotary selector

Replaces tabs for the type switch, in one row about as tall as tabs. Press a label to pick it, or press the knob to turn it to the next position. The lamp lights the chosen one; arrow keys move it.

Fallback: lit tab switches

Knobs

Knobs appear only where they choose something: the type and the format. The red knob has a red fin across a round cap, with the white pointer on one end of the fin; grey and blue knobs have a silver ring with a black line and a scalloped cap with a white tick, and the two turn together. The type switch is red, the format switch blue. No marks are printed around a knob. The pointer turns in 0.3 s.

Format selector

What each format carries

The dashed line is 20 kHz, the top of human hearing. The curves are approximate; they explain the formats, they don't measure the files.

Meters

The app has one meter, for the wait while a source is rate limited. It has a numbered scale, and the text under it says what it measures. Download progress shows on a display and on the track lights: green done, amber now, red failed, dark waiting.

Download progress

01Night FerryMira Holt · Qobuz · Max

Each element shows one thing and says what. Compact views show the cover, a display with tracks done and the current track, and one light per track. Only the detail view adds a picture: the disc being cut, standing on the panel without a frame. Its display shows tracks done and the time, and the track list shows each track, so nothing repeats. A queued download is a boxed tape with its position number.
