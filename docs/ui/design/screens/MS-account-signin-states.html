Sign-in states

The display names the app, shows the state and is the status region, so screen readers announce it. A failure turns it red and is an alert. A soft stop turns it amber. The unit has no title or lamp of its own. A state adds a line of text only to say what to do next.

1 · Checking
MUSIC SHELF
CHECKING
TIKHON

On load the same unit shows CHECKING ACCESS until Shelf knows whether you are signed in.

2 · Wrong username or password
MUSIC SHELF
WRONG USERNAME
OR PASSWORD

The display turns red and screen readers hear it at once. The password empties and takes focus.

3 · Too many tries
MUSIC SHELF
TOO MANY TRIES
TRY AGAIN LATER

After five wrong passwords a username gets one more try every twelve seconds. Shelf says so in its own words.

4 · No answer
MUSIC SHELF
NO ANSWER
TRY AGAIN

The server did not answer the access check, so there is no form yet. Try again asks once more.

5 · Waiting for your passkey
MUSIC SHELF
WAITING FOR
YOUR PASSKEY

The slide stays at ON while the browser asks for the passkey. The key turns only when the passkey signs in. Sliding back cancels.

6 · Passkey canceled
MUSIC SHELF
PASSKEY CANCELED
USE THE PASSWORD

The display turns amber, and the slide springs back to OFF.

7 · Signed in
MUSIC SHELF
HELLO, TIKHON
OPENING THE LIBRARY
22:41

After a passkey the slide and the key stay at ON. The three lamps light 260 ms apart and the clock starts, then the app opens with its menubar. With reduced motion it opens at once.

8 · A browser without passkeys
MUSIC SHELF
SIGNED OUT
PASSWORD

The key switch, the slide and their divider are gone, and the display asks only for the password.

9 · Session ended, over the app

Sign in again

SESSION ENDED
3 EDITS KEPT
Not tikhon?

A dialog keeps its title, since the app behind it is still there. The username stays filled in. Not tikhon? empties it, and another account reloads the page. The app behind keeps its staged edits.

10 · No accounts yet
MUSIC SHELF
NO ACCOUNTS YET
CREATE THE FIRST ONE

Shows while the server has no accounts. The new account signs in at once. The display turns red with PASSWORDS DIFFER, 8 CHARACTERS OR MORE, or USERNAME TAKEN when someone else created the first account a moment earlier.

11 · Phone, with the keyboard open
MUSIC SHELF
SIGNED OUT
PASSWORD OR PASSKEY

While the keyboard is open the unit moves to the top and the Passkey row scrolls under it, so Sign in stays above the keys. It goes back to the center when the keyboard closes.
